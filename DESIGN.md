# DESIGN SYSTEM: काय Jevnar? (Campus Fast Food & Canteen App)

Visual Archetype: Neo-brutalist Pop / Retro Fast Food / High-Contrast Chunky UI
Reference: bitejoy.webflow.io

---

## 1. Color Palette & Tokens
- Canvas Background: `--bg-yellow`: #FED97C (Solid Butterscotch Yellow)
- Primary Accent: `--accent-red`: #F04521 (Vivid Pop Red-Orange)
- High Contrast Stroke & Text: `--black`: #000000 (Pure Solid Black)
- Card Surfaces: `--white`: #FFFFFF
- Card Color Themes:
  - Red Card: #FF4D4D
  - Yellow Card: #FFD166
  - Cyan Sky Card: #4CC9F0
  - Pink Card: #FFB5A7
- Kitchen Admin Status Accents:
  - New / Placed: #F59E0B (Amber)
  - Preparing / Cooking: #2563EB (Electric Blue)
  - Ready for Pickup: #059669 (Emerald Green)

---

## 2. Geometry, Borders & Shadows
- Stroke: 3px to 4px solid #000000
- Neo-Brutalist Shadow: `box-shadow: 4px 4px 0px #000000;`
- Active / Pressed State: `transform: translate(3px, 3px); box-shadow: none;`
- Pill Radii: `border-radius: 100px;` (buttons, category tabs, badges)
- Card Radii: `border-radius: 24px;` (product cards, modal sheets)

---

## 3. Typography
- Display Title: Ultra-bold Condensed Vintage Sans (Realce / Impact style)
- Script Accent: Bubbly 3D Pop Cartoon Font with Black Stroke (Mentimun font style)
- Body & Labels: Modern Geometric Sans (Inter / Plus Jakarta Sans), Weights 700–900

---

## 4. Brand Identity
- App Name: "काय Jevnar?"
- Logo: Chef hat on "काय" with double yellow outline + bubbly red 3D "Jevnar?" with sparkles.
- Tagline: "Instant Campus Canteen • Zero Waiting Lines"
